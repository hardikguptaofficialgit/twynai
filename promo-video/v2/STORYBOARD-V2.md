## Product-truth guardrails

- The public persona, identity, knowledge, chat and booking states use synthetic local `*.demo.invalid` data and the isolated temporary SQLite database.
- The voice interface shown is the real Talk UI. No live speech recognition, Timbre synthesis, cloned voice, or paid provider response is claimed as recorded in this cut.
- Gnani Prisma STT, Timbre TTS and streaming are cited only as verified source integrations; verified provider names appear as text labels, not sponsor imagery.
