# Twyn V2 social caption

You can’t be everywhere. Your Twyn can.

Twyn helps creators shape an AI version of themselves from their identity, knowledge and voice—so visitors can discover, chat, and start a conversation from one link.

For voice, Twyn integrates Gnani Prisma for speech recognition and Gnani Timbre for text-to-speech, with streaming support in the application’s voice pipeline. The film uses the real Twyn UI and an isolated local demo persona.
