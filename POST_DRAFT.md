## LinkedIn
I built Twyn AI for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by @Gnani AI.

Twyn AI is your AI persona on a link. Share twyn.ai/yourname and anyone can chat with it or call it. It answers from your own notes and docs, speaks in your cloned voice, follows the caller's language (Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil and more, no language picker), and books a slot straight into your calendar.

Built on Gnani:
- Prisma v3 STT with indic-auto + code-mix for callers who switch languages mid-sentence
- Timbre v3 streaming TTS, so the first word plays in about 2 seconds
- Gnani voice cloning, so the persona sounds like you

Stack: Next.js, FastAPI with WebSockets, RAG with multilingual embeddings, Groq for reasoning.

Solo build, 2nd-year CS at KIIT. Would love your feedback, and a like or comment helps a lot.
