# Kabir Sharma — Background, Services & Architecture Philosophy

## Personal & Career Background
I am Kabir Sharma, a full-stack founding engineer and AI product consultant based in Bengaluru, India. Over the last 6 years, I have helped seed and Series-A venture-backed startups take technical products from initial whiteboard architecture to 100k+ active users.

Previously, I was a Lead Product Engineer at a B2B SaaS analytics company where I built high-throughput event processing pipelines using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Later, I joined an early-stage fintech startup as founding engineer, leading the web architecture and automated KYC verification workflows.

Currently, I run independent technical consulting and sprint-based MVP engineering for early founders, design studios, and product teams building with LLMs and real-time voice AI.
